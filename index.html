Questions

Before you start

Is the self-check really free?

Yes. It's free, takes about five minutes, and needs no account. Your results appear on the page as soon as you finish.

Is this a scientific or validated EQ test?

No. It's a reflection tool: twelve plain-language statements to help you notice your own habits. It is not a validated psychometric, clinical or diagnostic assessment, and it doesn't label or rank you.

What happens to my answers?

They stay in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to email your results to yourself through the form, and then only what you type in.

Is this therapy or counseling?

No. This is leadership development. If you are struggling with your mental health, please reach out to a licensed professional.

Do you work with teams and organizations?

Yes, that's what the cohorts, workshops and industry-specific programs are for. Programs are in development. Join the waitlist from your results, or book a free strategy conversation to talk about your team.

Will I be pressured to buy something?

No. The self-check and the strategy conversation are free. Program details, including any fees, are shared before you commit to anything.

Which industries do you focus on?

Healthcare, technology and finance, where performance, pressure and people intersect most. Leaders from other fields are welcome to take the self-check too.
